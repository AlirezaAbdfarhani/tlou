ABOUT THE GAME

THE LAST OF US PART I
When everything you love is taken away, what is left to live for?
The world did not end in a single moment. It fell apart piece by piece, as an infectious outbreak spread across the planet and transformed familiar cities into abandoned ruins. Governments collapsed, communities disappeared, and the survivors were forced to build their lives around fear, scarcity, and the constant threat of death. Twenty years later, nature has begun reclaiming the remains of civilization. Streets lie beneath layers of vegetation, skyscrapers stand as silent monuments to a forgotten world, and humanity struggles to survive among the ruins of everything it once built.
But before the world changed, Joel Miller was simply a father.
He had a home, a job, and a daughter named Sarah. Their lives were far from perfect, but they shared something that the apocalypse would eventually take from millions of people: the ordinary moments that make life meaningful. A birthday present, a conversation at home, a familiar voice, and the comfort of knowing that tomorrow might look much like today. When the outbreak reaches their hometown, Joel and Sarah are forced to flee into the chaos. Within a single devastating night, Joel experiences a loss that will define the rest of his life. Sarah's death leaves a wound that twenty years of survival cannot truly heal.
The man who emerges from that night is no longer the father he once was. Hardened by grief and shaped by a world that rewards cruelty, Joel learns to keep his emotions buried beneath a wall of anger, distrust, and determination. In the quarantine zones, survival becomes his only reliable purpose. He has learned that caring about someone can make them vulnerable, that trust can become a weakness, and that the past is often easier to endure when it remains unspoken. Everything changes when Joel is given a mission involving Ellie, a fourteen-year-old girl whose immunity to the infection could hold the key to humanity's future. What initially seems like another dangerous assignment soon becomes a journey across a devastated America. Accompanied by people whose lives intersect with theirs in unexpected ways, Joel and Ellie travel through abandoned cities, ruined suburbs, dense forests, and isolated settlements, encountering both the infected and the desperate survivors who have learned to fear one another as much as the world itself.
As their journey continues, the relationship between Joel and Ellie gradually changes. Their conversations become more personal, their silences more meaningful, and their growing trust begins to challenge the emotional distance Joel has maintained for two decades. Ellie is not Sarah, and nothing can replace the daughter he lost. Yet her presence brings something back into Joel's life that he believed had disappeared forever: the possibility of caring about another person without constantly thinking about what it might cost him.
Along the way, the world reveals its many contradictions. Beautiful landscapes exist beside horrifying reminders of the outbreak. Strangers offer kindness when it is least expected, while others commit terrible acts in the name of survival. Every encounter forces Joel and Ellie to confront difficult questions about morality, sacrifice, and the fragile value of human life. In this world, surviving is not always the same as living, and doing what seems necessary does not always mean doing what is right.
The Last of Us Part I is a deeply personal story about grief, fatherhood, trust, and the lengths people will go to protect those they love. Its emotional power comes not only from the dangers that threaten its characters but from the quiet moments between them: a conversation in an abandoned building, a joke shared during a dangerous journey, a glimpse of beauty in a dying world, or a memory that refuses to fade.
At its heart, this is the story of two people who have lost more than they know how to express. One is a man who believes his ability to love has died with his daughter. The other is a girl who has grown up in a world where the future has never been promised. Together, they discover that human connection can survive even when almost everything else has fallen apart.
But when their journey finally reaches its destination, Joel must face a decision that brings his past, his grief, and his love into conflict with the future of humanity itself. The answer will not simply determine what happens next. It will reveal who Joel has become, what Ellie means to him, and whether the world deserves the sacrifice it demands.
Somewhere between the ruins of the old world and the uncertainty of the new, a broken man finds something worth protecting again. And this time, he may be unwilling to lose it.
